# yahayuta

DX engineer who turns conversations with business teams into systems people actually use.
I build data platforms and generative-AI workflows, from requirements through operations.

## What I do

- **Data platforms**: build and run analytics foundations on BigQuery, dbt, TROCCO and Terraform, with dashboards in Looker Studio.
- **Generative AI at work**: design business AI with Claude (MCP), Dify and n8n. I choose models, analyze wrong answers, and shape outputs so teams keep using them.
- **E-commerce integrations**: develop and maintain integrations between multi-channel e-commerce platforms and order management systems.
- **Teams and enablement**: led maintenance teams and offshore development (in English), and help non-engineers build their own tools.

## Tech

- **Languages**: Python, Java, PHP, JavaScript / TypeScript, SQL
- **Data & Cloud**: BigQuery, dbt, Terraform, Google Cloud, AWS
- **AI**: Claude (MCP), Dify, n8n, OpenAI, Gemini
